Frozen research assets accompanying the manuscript Trajectory-based model-input interventions and reference-dependent player attribution in football by Fang Chen and Jiqian Lin. Version v2026.11.19 contains 17 unchanged archives of source code, public runtime bindings, source-attributed derived inputs, clean inference weights, full non-pickle replay references, and unrounded statistical outputs.

Validation is bounded: eight clean-only entry/preflight or saved-statistic routes passed with 3,089 materialized inputs and no original-workspace fallback; selected saved statistics reproduced byte-for-byte. The clean-weight reader checks covered 21 reader families and 11,345 arrays. The new diagnostic bridge was checked only on fixed prefix rows 0 and 1, five frozen seeds plus CV and six paths, with same-batch exact equality. This is not a demonstrated complete raw-data-to-training-to-paper rerun or a cross-platform certification.

The frozen four-match diagnostic retains all 4,992 historically admitted, future-qualified windows; these matches were previously exposed, not a new blind test. All seed, fixture, negative, rejected and NA results remain. Factual prediction improvements do not identify real intervention benefits or causal player credit. Earlier reconstruction, transfer, Metrica and adverse ball results remain available. Rows, windows and seeds are not independent matches; seed SD describes variation across the five seeds.
