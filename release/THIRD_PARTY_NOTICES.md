# Source attribution and licence boundaries

The release contains author material and source-specific derivatives. It has no blanket licence covering all bytes. The full author MIT text is in `LICENSE`; eligible author-owned weights, documentation and statistics use [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). `license_scope.json` identifies package and subtree exceptions. Old proposal/pending text inside immutable archives records their preparation state; this release-level grant supersedes that text only for the identified author-owned material.

## IDSSE

Credit Bassek M, Rein R, Weber H and Memmert D, *An integrated dataset of spatiotemporal and event data in elite soccer*, Figshare v1 (2025), [doi:10.6084/m9.figshare.28196177.v1](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.28196177.v1). Data owner: Deutsche Fussball Liga; collected by Sportec Solutions. The pinned analysis mirror revision is `a715a38dfbaf5f58e431727c2b78d174101a703c`. Released source data retain CC BY 4.0. Derivations include fixed histories/masks, normalized coordinates, clock and recorded-shot labels, generated features, saved predictions and terminal draws. Retain attribution and identify later changes.

Credit SkillCorner Open Data, in collaboration with PySport, [pinned revision 4340d274572876239c154c90bc507a9b3250a656](https://github.com/SkillCorner/opendata/tree/4340d274572876239c154c90bc507a9b3250a656). Processing includes fixed source cohorts, prefixes, masks, commands and diagnostics. This notice makes no grant for outside video or commercial products.

## SoccerTrack

Credit the [SoccerTrack v2 dataset creators](https://huggingface.co/datasets/atomscott/soccertrack-v2) and retain the pinned `LICENSE-DATA` notice. The recorded release v1.2, revision `325bd4c57ee8e135ab652f3921d274526ada496b`, has CC BY 4.0 evidence for its stated GSR/BAS scope. The source file version remains distinct. This release includes source checks and analyses, not an added raw dataset mirror. It does not authenticate historical legacy tracker files.

## Metrica Sports

Credit [Metrica Sports Sample Data](https://github.com/metrica-sports/sample-data). Included material is nonreconstructive analytical summaries; tracking and event records must be obtained through the provider route. The author licence does not grant rights over provider-constrained material. A folder name does not establish which data trained a model.

## StatsBomb

Credit [StatsBomb Open Data](https://github.com/statsbomb/open-data), now redirected by the provider to [hudl/open-data](https://github.com/hudl/open-data). The retained source agreement and README are in the underlying-results archive at `payload/source_notices/004_statsbomb_fixed_LICENSE_readable.txt` and `005_statsbomb_fixed_README.md`. The `payload/body_goal13/` analysis subtree is excluded from the author MIT/CC BY grant. The retained agreement permits sharing analysis subject to its terms, restricts commercial use of data and derived analysis, and requires StatsBomb brand-logo accreditation. Obtain official branding through the [StatsBomb media pack](https://statsbomb.com/media-pack/). No raw events, lineups, per-event predictions or StatsBomb-trained weights are added here. These conclusions are the authors’ analyses and do not imply StatsBomb endorsement.

## Historical GSR / SoccerNet-labelled analyses

Acquisition, exact source identity and ball lineage remain unresolved for the historical files. Only nonreconstructive analyses, hashes and methods are within this release scope. No current provider licence is used to authenticate or grant redistribution rights to those older records. The `body_legacy50` and mixed-coordinate `S7` boundaries remain explicit.
